Children who go hungry
Why we should eat
A child who eats well can think well, play well and dream big. Every meal is fuel for tomorrow.

Growing bodies and busy minds need energy, protein and vitamins every day. Skipping meals leaves us tired, distracted and weak. Eating on time, and eating a full plate, is how we study better, stay healthy and grow strong.

Energyfor classes, sports and growing bodies
Focusa fed mind learns and remembers more
Why we should not waste
Every grain on the plate is a farmer's months of work. Wasting it forgets someone who went without.

While some of us leave food on the plate, millions of children go to bed hungry. Take only what you can eat, finish what you take, and give thanks for it. It is a small act of gratitude we can repeat three times a day.

733 millionpeople faced hunger in 2023, about 1 in 11 worldwide
1.05 billiontonnes of food were wasted in 2022
Sources: UN FAO State of Food Security and Nutrition 2024; UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024.
